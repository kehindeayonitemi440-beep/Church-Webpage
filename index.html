Our story

Faith that becomes a way of life.

Christ Gospel Apostolic Church exists to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and encourage believers to live out their faith with love, integrity and purpose.

CHRIST GOSPEL APOSTOLIC CHURCH(CGAC) was founded on November 2, 1947 at Oke-Foko Ibadan,Nigeria by Apostle Peter Akinloye Olatunji.

Lagos Expansion: In 1962, Apostle Paul Oladejo Okedoyin was led by a divine call to plant the church in Mushin, Lagos, eventually expanding the Lagos area and overseas missions.
Assemblies: By 2026, the church has grown to encompass approximately 70 active assemblies across Nigerian states (including Lagos, Ogun, Kwara, Oyo, and Abuja) as well as international outposts in the United States and the United Kingdom. The faith of CGAC is anchored on several fundamental biblical tenets:
* The Holy Scriptures: Believed to be the infallible, authoritative Word of God, serving as the supreme guide for salvation and Christian living.
* The Triune God: Absolute belief in the coeternal, coequal Trinity—Father, Son (Jesus Christ), and Holy Spirit.
* Salvation and Baptism: Salvation is taught as coming exclusively by grace through faith in Christ, preceded by repentance. The church practices total water immersion for believers.
* The Holy Spirit: Heavy emphasis is placed on the Baptism of the Holy Spirit and the continuation of the nine spiritual gifts for empowering the modern church.
